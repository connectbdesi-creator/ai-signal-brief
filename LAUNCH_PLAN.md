# AI Signal Brief Launch Plan

This is the beginner operating plan for turning AI Signal Brief into a useful public channel and, later, a small paid product.

## Phase 1: Fix Distribution

Goal: make sure people can receive the brief without visiting the website.

1. Fix `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` in GitHub Secrets.
2. Confirm the bot receives one workflow digest.
3. Create a public Telegram channel named `AI Signal Brief`.
4. Add the bot as an admin if you want the automation to post there later.
5. Share the website link and Telegram channel link with 10-20 trusted people.

## Phase 2: Publish Daily

Goal: build trust before monetizing.

Daily routine:

- Morning: post top 5 AI updates.
- Afternoon: post model/tool launches.
- Night: post "what mattered today".

Weekly routine:

- Sunday: post "AI Week in 7 Updates".
- Ask readers what they want more of: models, tools, research, policy, business.

## Phase 3: Add Social Growth

Start with Instagram before YouTube Shorts because it is easier to produce consistently.

Instagram format:

- 1 carousel per day.
- Slide 1: "5 AI updates you missed today"
- Slides 2-6: one update per slide.
- Final slide: "Follow AI Signal Brief for the 2-hour AI radar."

YouTube Shorts format:

- 30-45 seconds.
- Hook: "Three AI updates that matter today."
- 3 updates.
- Ending: "Full brief is on AI Signal Brief."

## Phase 4: Monetize

Do not start with platform ads. Start with direct monetization.

Free offer:

- Public website.
- Public Telegram brief.
- Weekly highlights.

Paid team offer:

- Private Telegram group.
- Daily executive summary.
- Weekly "what matters for business" recap.
- Custom categories.

Pilot pricing:

- Team channel: `$49/month`
- Sponsor slot: `$99/week`

Raise prices only after you have proof that people read it and ask for more.

## Phase 5: Improve Product

Useful upgrades:

- Daily 9 AM executive summary.
- Separate feeds for founders, developers, marketers, and investors.
- Email newsletter.
- Sponsor tracking links.
- Submission form for AI tool builders.
- Paid archive and trend reports.

## Questions To Answer

Before launching publicly:

1. What public contact email should be on the website?
2. Do you want this to be personal-brand led or a standalone media brand?
3. Should the first audience be founders, developers, marketers, or general AI learners?
4. Do you want Telegram only, or Telegram plus email newsletter?
